Black Bean Soup

Ingredients:
2 celery stalks
1 onion
2 cloves garlic
1 teaspoon smoked paprika
1 teaspoon cumin
1 teaspoon salt
4 cups cooked black beans
4 cups (1 box) chicken broth

1. chop the celery and onion. Peal the garlic (see Tip, below) and finely chop it. 
2. Heat a large pot on medium heat until hot. Add a small amount oil, just enough to cover the bottom of the pan. Add the onions and celery and saute until they are golden, about 7-10 minutes.
3. Clear a small area in the bottom of the pot and add the garlic in this area. Fry about 15-30 seconds, until the garlic is fragrant. Add the cumin and paprika and stir. 
4. Add the broth and black beans. Bring to a gentle boil, cover with a lid, and simmer for 10 minutes.
5. Use an immersion blender to blend the beans. Blend until pretty smoot.


(Tip: cut the stem end of the unpealed garlic off, then press each clove with the flat side of a knife, just enough to crush the clove inside the skin. The skin will easily remove after you crush the clove.)
